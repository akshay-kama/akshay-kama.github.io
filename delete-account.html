Account Deletion

This page explains how Poker Chips users can delete their account and associated data.

How It Works

Delete directly in the app.

Poker Chips creates an anonymous account automatically using Firebase Anonymous Authentication when the app is first used. Users do not create a username or password.

To delete your account and associated data, open the app and go to About/Support, then select Delete My Data.

This deletes the anonymous account and associated Poker Chips data tied to that account.

What Gets Deleted

Associated app data removed with the account.

  • The Firebase anonymous account used by Poker Chips on that device.
  • Hosted tables owned by that account.
  • Owned player records associated with that account.
  • Related feed records associated with those owned records.

No additional retention period is intended after deletion completes in the app.
